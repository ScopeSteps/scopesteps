# Bike Repair Workshop Sign-up: Project Brief

Prepared by the Riverside Community Centre. Synthetic example; all names are fictional.

## Background

Volunteers run a bike repair workshop every Saturday. People sign up on a paper sheet at the front desk, the sheet is often full by Tuesday, and nobody knows how many repair stands are free until they arrive.

## Goals

- Let residents see free repair slots and book one themselves.
- Stop more people turning up than there are repair stands.
- Give the workshop lead one list of who is coming each Saturday.

## In scope for the first release

- A resident can book one 45-minute slot per Saturday.
- The workshop lead can set how many repair stands are open each week.
- A resident can cancel their own booking.

## Out of scope

- Payments and donations.
- Ordering spare parts.

## Open points

We have not decided how late a resident may cancel. The workshop lead will raise it at the kickoff meeting.
