# Clinic Appointment Reminders: Project Brief

Prepared by Riverside Family Clinic. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 5 October 2026.

## Background

Riverside Family Clinic sees about 90 patients a day. One in ten patients misses their appointment without telling us, and the front desk spends every morning phoning people to confirm.

## What we need

The clinic sends each patient a text message reminder two days before their appointment.

A patient can reply YES to confirm or NO to cancel, and the appointment list shows each reply.

When a patient cancels by text, the slot becomes free in the booking calendar straight away.

## Out of scope

Booking new appointments by text is not part of this project.
