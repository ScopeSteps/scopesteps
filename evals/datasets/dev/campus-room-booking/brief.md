# Campus Room Booking: Project Brief

Prepared by the Facilities Office, Northfield College. Synthetic example; all names are fictional. Date: 2 September 2026.

## Background

Students and staff book the 24 group-study rooms in the library by emailing the Facilities Office. Requests are tracked in a shared spreadsheet, double bookings happen several times a week, and nobody can see which rooms are free without asking.

## Goals

- Let students and staff see which study rooms are free and book one themselves.
- Reduce double bookings to zero.
- Give the Facilities Office a single view of all bookings.

## In scope for the first release

- Students and staff sign in with their existing college account.
- A user can search rooms by date, time and capacity, and book an individual one-off slot.
- A booking can last up to 4 hours.
- The Facilities Office can block a room for maintenance.

## Open points

We have not yet agreed what happens when someone cannot attend a booking. The Facilities Office will discuss this with Student Services.
