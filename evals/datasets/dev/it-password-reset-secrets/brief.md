# Self-Service Password Reset: Project Brief

Prepared by Calder County Council IT. Synthetic example; all names, organisations and credentials are fictional. Date: 4 November 2026.

## Background

The service desk resets about 400 staff passwords a month by phone. Each call takes eight minutes, and callers are identified only by their name and office.

## What we need

Staff reset their own password from a web page after confirming a code sent to their work mobile.

A reset locks for one hour after five wrong codes in a row, and the service desk is told.

Every reset is written to the security log with the time and the staff number, never the new password.

## Out of scope

Resetting passwords for external contractors is not part of this project.
