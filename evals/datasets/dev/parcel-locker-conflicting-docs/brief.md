# Parcel Locker Network: Project Brief

Prepared by Brightway Couriers. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 10 November 2026.

## Background

Brightway is placing 120 parcel lockers at stations and supermarkets. Customers who miss a delivery today must collect from a depot.

## What we need

A customer can choose a locker as the delivery address when they order.

The customer gets a text with a collection code when the parcel is in the locker.

## Out of scope

Sending parcels from a locker is not part of this project.
