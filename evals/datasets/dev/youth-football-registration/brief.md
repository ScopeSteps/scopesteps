# Youth Football Registration: Project Brief

Prepared by Eastfield Juniors Football Club. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 30 September 2026.

## Background

Eastfield Juniors runs eleven teams from under-8 to under-16, with about 220 players and 30 volunteer coaches. Registration happens on paper forms at the start of each season, fees are collected in cash, and team lists live in each coach's phone. Every September the club loses two weekends to sorting forms, and last season three players turned up to the wrong pitch.

## Goals

- Let parents register their children online without a paper form.
- Give coaches one up-to-date list of their squad and fixtures.
- Stop players missing matches because of out-of-date information.

## In scope for the first release

Parents create an account with their email address and a password, and sign in with it. Each child's record holds their name, date of birth, an emergency contact number and any medical notes the parent wants coaches to see.

The fixtures page shows every match and training session for the next four weeks with the team, kick-off time, pitch and whether it is home or away. A parent can register a child for any team in the child's age group, and the system stops registrations once a team's squad is full.

Parents can withdraw a child and get a refund up to 7 days after registering.

Coaches can add, move and cancel fixtures for their own team, and see the squad list with medical notes. When a coach cancels a fixture, every parent in that squad gets an email.

## Not in the first release

Coach safeguarding checks stay with the national football association's system; we do not store or check them. Selling club kit online is also out of scope.

## Notes for the team

The site has to work well on phones, because most parents will use it at the side of the pitch.
