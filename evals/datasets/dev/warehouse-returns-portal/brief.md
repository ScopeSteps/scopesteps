# Warehouse Returns Portal: Project Brief

Prepared by Northgate Outdoor Supplies. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 2 October 2026.

## Background

Northgate sells camping and hiking gear online and ships from one warehouse. We handle about 1,400 returns a month. Today customers email the support team, an agent types each return into a spreadsheet, and the warehouse team checks the spreadsheet twice a day. Returns wait four days on average before anyone opens the parcel, and refunds take up to three weeks.

## Goals

- Let customers start a return themselves.
- Give the warehouse team a live list of parcels that are on their way back.
- Cut the time from parcel arrival to refund.

## In scope

Customers start a return from their order history by choosing the items and a reason from a fixed list. Customers can return items up to 30 days after delivery.

After a customer starts a return, the portal emails them a prepaid shipping label and a return number.

The warehouse team sees a list of expected returns with the return number, items, reason and the date the label was created. When a parcel arrives, a warehouse operator scans the return number and records the condition of each item as new, opened or damaged.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the operator has recorded the condition of every item in the return.

## Not in scope

Exchanges for a different size or colour are not part of this project; customers will keep placing a new order. Returns from our two physical shops are also out of scope.

## Decision rights

Hannah Osei, head of customer operations, owns the returns policy. Changes to refund rules are Hannah's decision.
