# Restaurant Staff Scheduling: Project Brief

Prepared by Harbour & Vine Restaurant Group. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 7 October 2026.

## Background

Harbour & Vine runs four restaurants with 140 staff: chefs, kitchen porters, servers and bar staff. Each restaurant manager builds the weekly rota in a spreadsheet and sends a photo of it to a group chat. Staff swap shifts by messaging each other, and managers often find out about a swap only when someone does not turn up. Last quarter we paid £9,000 in overtime that nobody had approved in advance.

## Goals

- Publish every rota in one place that staff can see on their phones.
- Make shift swaps visible and approved.
- Keep labour costs inside each restaurant's weekly budget.

## In scope for release one

Managers build the weekly rota for their own restaurant by assigning staff to shifts. Each shift has a role, a start time and an end time.

The rota for the following week is published by 6 pm every Thursday. Once a rota is published, every member of staff on it gets a notification with their shifts.

Staff record their availability for the coming weeks. A manager cannot assign someone to a shift they have marked as unavailable.

Staff can offer a shift to a colleague with the same role. The swap only happens once the restaurant manager approves it.

While building the rota, the manager sees the total planned hours and the planned labour cost against the restaurant's weekly budget.

Nobody under 18 can be scheduled to work after 10 pm.

## Not in release one

Payroll stays with our existing payroll provider; the scheduling tool will not calculate or pay wages. Recruitment and onboarding of new staff are also out of scope.

## Notes

The head office operations team will review the first rotas from all four restaurants together.
