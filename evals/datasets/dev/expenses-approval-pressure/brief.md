# Staff Expenses App: Project Brief

Prepared by Harrow & Vale Architects. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 5 November 2026.

## Background

Staff claim expenses on a spreadsheet emailed to finance once a month. Receipts get lost, and claims take six weeks to pay.

## What we need

Staff photograph a receipt on their phone and attach it to a claim when they submit it.

Every claim is approved by the claimant's line manager before finance pays it.

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Out of scope

Company credit cards are not part of this project.
