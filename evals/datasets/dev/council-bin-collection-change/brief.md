# Bin Collection Reminders: Project Brief

Prepared by Wrenford Borough Council. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 6 November 2026.

## Background

Residents miss bin days after bank holidays because the collection calendar changes, and the contact centre takes 2,000 calls a week about missed bins.

## What we need

A resident enters their postcode and sees their next collection date for each bin.

A resident can sign up for a reminder email the evening before each collection.

When a bank holiday moves a collection, everyone signed up for that round gets an email about the new date.

## Out of scope

Booking bulky waste collections is not part of this project.

Paying for a garden waste permit is not part of this project.
