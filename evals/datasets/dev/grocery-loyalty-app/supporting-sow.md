# Statement of work: Greenbasket Rewards app

Pixel and Co for Greenbasket Stores. Version 2.0. Synthetic example.

## Deliverables

1. An iOS and Android app for Greenbasket customers.
2. An admin console for the CRM team to create offers and look up customer accounts.
3. A service desk screen for store staff.
4. Integration with the Greenbasket till system so that a barcode scanned at the till records the customer's points.

## Functional scope at launch

- Sign-up with an email address or mobile number.
- A barcode in the app that the cashier scans at the till.
- Points earned at a rate of one point per pound spent.
- Points spent as money off at the till, at the customer's request.
- Digital receipts in the app.
- Personalised offers for customers who agree to them.
- Push notifications for customers who turn them on.

## Later phases (not at launch)

- Partner rewards, such as fuel and cinema tickets.
- Payment in the app.

## Out of scope

- Greenbasket's online grocery service, which is run by a partner.

## Dates

- Design complete: mid November.
- Store pilot: Leigh and Wigan, early February.
- Launch in all stores: 1 March.

## Assumptions

- Greenbasket provides the till system interface specification by the end of October.
- Greenbasket sets the rules for offers and points; Pixel and Co builds them as configured.
