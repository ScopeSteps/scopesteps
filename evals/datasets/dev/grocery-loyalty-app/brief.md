# Project brief: Greenbasket Rewards app

Prepared by the Marketing team at Greenbasket Stores. Synthetic example; all names, places and figures are fictional.

## Background

Greenbasket runs 46 grocery stores across the north west. Our paper stamp card was withdrawn two years ago and we have had no loyalty scheme since. Competitors with apps are winning our younger shoppers, and we know almost nothing about who shops with us or how often.

## Goals

- Launch a free loyalty app that rewards customers for shopping with us.
- Understand our customers better, with their consent, so offers are relevant to them.
- Replace paper receipts for customers who want that.
- Grow the number of customers who shop with us at least once a week.

## Scope

The app is for shoppers in our stores. Customers collect points when they scan the app at the till and spend them as money off a future shop. Our online grocery service is run by a partner and is not part of this project.

## Constraints

- The app launches in all stores on 1 March.
- The budget is 520,000 pounds for the build and the first year of running costs, plus the cost of the points themselves, which finance will set.
- Customer data is handled under UK data protection law, and marketing uses only data customers have agreed to.

## Who is involved

The sponsor is the Marketing Director. The CRM team runs the project with store operations, finance, the data protection officer and our agency, Pixel and Co, who build the app.
