# City Bike Hire App: Project Brief

Prepared by Kelso City Transport. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 8 November 2026.

## Background

Riders hire bikes at 40 docking stations by tapping a card at the dock. There is no app, so riders cannot see whether a dock has bikes before they walk to it.

## What we need

Riders see a live map of docking stations with the number of free bikes and free spaces at each.

A rider can reserve a bike at a station for up to fifteen minutes before collecting it.

## Out of scope

Electric bikes are not part of this project.
