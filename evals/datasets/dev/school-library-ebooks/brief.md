# School Library E-books: Project Brief

Prepared by Lakeside Secondary School. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 9 October 2026.

## Background

Lakeside Secondary has 900 students and one librarian. The library bought 300 e-book licences this year, but students can only read them on the library computers. Most students never visit the library at lunchtime because the queue is too long.

## What we want

Students sign in with their school account and borrow an e-book on any device.

A student can have at most three e-books on loan at a time, and each loan lasts 14 days.

When every licence for a title is on loan, students can join a waiting list and are emailed when a copy becomes free.

The librarian sees which titles are borrowed most each month.

The reading app should be engaging and modern.

## Not included

Printed books stay in the current library system and are not part of this project.
