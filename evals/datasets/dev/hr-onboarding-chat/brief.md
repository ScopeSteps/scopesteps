# New Starter Onboarding: Project Brief

Prepared by Lumen Logistics People Team. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 7 November 2026.

## Background

New starters wait up to two weeks for a laptop and building pass, and managers chase five departments by email to set them up.

## What we need

When HR enters a new starter, IT, facilities and payroll each get a task to complete before the start date.

The hiring manager sees a checklist showing which tasks are done and which are late.

A task not done three days before the start date is flagged as late and its department is reminded.

## Out of scope

Recruitment and job adverts are not part of this project.
