# Pharmacy Home Delivery: Project Brief

Prepared by Northgate Pharmacy. Synthetic example; all names, addresses and numbers are fictional. Date: 2 November 2026.

## Background

Northgate Pharmacy delivers repeat prescriptions to about 300 housebound patients a month. Drivers work from a printed list, and patients phone the counter all day to ask when their delivery will arrive.

## What we need

Each patient gets a text message on the morning of their delivery giving a two-hour delivery window.

Drivers see their route for the day on a phone, with the stops in the order they should be visited.

A driver marks each delivery as delivered or not delivered, and a failed delivery is rebooked for the next working day.

## Out of scope

Taking payment at the door is not part of this project.
