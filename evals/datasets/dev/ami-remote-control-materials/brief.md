# Project brief: a new television remote control

Real Reaction, a consumer electronics company, wants a new remote control for televisions. It should be original, trendy and user friendly, and it should stand out from the remotes people already own.

The remote will sell for 25 Euro, and the company aims to make a profit of 50 million Euro on it. It will be sold on an international scale. Production must not cost more than 12.50 Euro per remote.

A team of four will design it: a project manager, a marketing expert, a user interface designer and an industrial designer. The work runs in three stages, functional design, conceptual design and detailed design, and each stage ends with a team meeting.

The company may send the team further requirements during the project.
