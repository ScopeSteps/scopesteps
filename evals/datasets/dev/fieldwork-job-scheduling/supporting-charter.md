# Project charter: Fieldwork

Harlow Water, Operations Change Team. Version 1.2, approved by the Head of Field Operations. Synthetic example.

## Purpose

Fieldwork replaces the dispatch spreadsheet and the paper job sheet with a mobile app for technicians and a web screen for the dispatch desk. It is the first step in the Field Operations plan to give customers firm appointment times.

## Objectives

1. Every technician starts the day with their jobs on their phone, without a phone call from the desk.
2. The dispatch desk knows the status of every job without ringing round.
3. Completed work reaches billing within one working day instead of around ten.
4. Customers are given an arrival window of two hours or less.

## In scope

- A mobile app for field technicians on the company phones they already carry.
- A web screen for the dispatch desk.
- Planned work and emergency jobs in the North and Central districts.
- A link to the billing system for completed jobs.

## Out of scope

- Timesheets, overtime claims and anything else that affects pay. These stay in the HR system.
- Route planning or automatic ordering of jobs.
- The South district, until release one has run for a full quarter.
- Customer self-service, such as rebooking online.

## Phasing

Release one is due by the end of March. Offline working, so that technicians can use the app without a mobile signal, is planned for phase two, after release one, because it needs changes to be synchronised in both directions.

| Sponsor          | Head of Field Operations   |

| Dispatch         | Dispatch supervisors       |
| Technician voice | Technician representatives |

The sponsor decides on scope, budget and dates. Changes to scope after approval go to the sponsor in writing.

## Risks

- Low mobile signal in parts of the North district may stop technicians using the app.
- Technicians may not trust the app if they think it is used to watch them.
- The asset register has no interface for other systems to update it.
