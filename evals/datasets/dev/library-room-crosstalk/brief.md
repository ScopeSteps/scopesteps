# Library Study Rooms: Project Brief

Prepared by Ashby University Library. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 9 November 2026.

## Background

The library has 24 group study rooms booked on a whiteboard at the help desk. Rooms sit empty while students queue for them.

## What we need

Students book a study room online with their university login.

A booking that nobody checks in to within fifteen minutes of its start is cancelled and the room is freed.

## Out of scope

Booking library computers is not part of this project.
