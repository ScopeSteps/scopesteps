# Welcome portal: HR requirements list (first draft)

People and Culture, drafted before the discovery workshop. Synthetic example.

This is our starting list of what HR needs from a new starter portal. It will change after the workshop.

## Before day one

1. The starter fills in their personal details: legal name, preferred name, address, date of birth and phone number.
2. The starter gives an emergency contact.
3. The starter gives their bank details and completes the tax starter checklist.
4. The starter uploads their right to work document. HR keeps a copy of the document in the portal for audit, as the Home Office may ask to see it.
5. The starter reads the code of conduct, the IT security policy and the data protection policy and confirms they have read them.
6. The starter sees a welcome page with the address of their office, their start time and the name of the person to ask for at reception.
7. The starter uploads a photo for their ID badge.

## For hiring managers

8. The manager gets a checklist of what to do before the starter arrives and in their first week.

## Not for this project

- Contract signing, which stays in the e-signature tool.
- Probation reviews, which stay in the HR system.

## Questions for the workshop

- Should contractors and temporary staff use the portal?
- Can we collect equal opportunities monitoring through the portal?
