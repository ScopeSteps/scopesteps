# Project brief: Welcome, the new starter portal

Prepared by People and Culture at Kestrel Insurance. Synthetic example; all names, places and figures are fictional.

## Background

Kestrel Insurance hires around 300 people a year, most of them into the claims and customer service teams. Today a new starter receives their contract and eight attachments by email, prints and signs forms, and brings them in on their first day. Bank details arrive on paper and are typed into payroll by hand. Laptops and system accounts are requested by the hiring manager, usually too late: a third of new starters spend their first morning without a working laptop.

Exit surveys show that people who leave within their first three months often mention a confusing first week.

## Goals

- Give every new starter one place to complete what we need from them before their first day.
- Get bank and tax details to payroll accurately, without retyping.
- Make sure every starter has a working laptop and accounts on their first morning.
- Give hiring managers a clear list of what they need to do before and during the first week.

## Scope

The portal is for new permanent employees who have accepted an offer. It opens once the offer is accepted and stays available for their first month. Contracts continue to be signed in the existing e-signature tool.

## Constraints

- Personal data is handled under the company's data protection policy and stays in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area.
- The portal must work on a personal phone or laptop, because starters do not have company equipment until day one.
- The first release should be live for the September intake.

## Who is involved

The sponsor is the HR Director. People and Culture runs the project with payroll, the IT service desk, IT security and a group of hiring managers. The HR systems team builds the portal on the company's existing HR platform.
