# Makerspace Tool Library: Project Brief

Prepared by Riverside Community Makerspace. Synthetic example; all names are fictional. Date: 15 September 2026.

## Background

The makerspace lends around 120 tools to members, from cordless drills to a tile cutter. Loans are recorded on paper cards at the front desk, tools go missing, and members cannot check whether a tool is available before they travel in.

## Goals

- Members can see which tools are available and request a loan online.
- Volunteers can check tools out and back in at the desk.
- Fewer tools go missing.

## First release

- Members sign in with the email address they registered with.
- A standard loan lasts 7 days.
- Power tools can only be borrowed by members who have completed the safety induction.
- Volunteers record check-out and return at the desk.

## Not included

Online payments and deposits are not part of the first release. The makerspace stays free for members.
