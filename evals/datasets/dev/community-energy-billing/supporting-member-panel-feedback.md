# Members' panel feedback on billing

Collected by Helen Price, members' panel, September 2026. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Comments are paraphrased and members are not named.

## How we collected it

The panel asked members at the summer open day and through a short survey in the newsletter. 41 members replied. Most were from supply households, but some share-only members also commented.

## What members said about the current bills

Twenty-six members said they could not tell from the bill when they had used electricity, or whether the time-of-day tariff was saving them money.

Fourteen members said the bill arrived at a different time each month, which made budgeting hard.

Nine members said they had never received a bill by email and had to ask for one. In most cases the email address held by the co-operative was out of date.

Six members said the bill used words they did not understand, such as "standing charge" and "kWh".

## What members asked for

A breakdown of usage and cost by time of day.

A daily usage chart.

The bill on the same day every month.

A way to update their email address and phone number themselves.

A short explanation of each term on the bill.

The option to pay by card.

## Other comments

Several members said they would like to see how much electricity the co-operative's panels generated each month, and how much of it they used themselves. Some asked whether the co-operative could show this on the bill.

Two members asked whether they could switch to a fixed tariff instead of the time-of-day tariff.

Many members said they valued the personal service from Gareth and hoped it would not be lost.

## Panel recommendation

The panel recommends that the first release concentrates on clear, regular bills, and leaves generation figures for a later release.
