# Leisure Centre Class Booking: Project Brief

Prepared by Eastfield Leisure Centre. Synthetic example; all names and organisations are fictional. Date: 3 November 2026.

## Background

Members book fitness classes at the front desk or by phone. Popular classes fill within minutes of opening, and staff keep the waiting lists on paper.

## What we need

Members book a place in a class online, up to seven days before it starts.

When a class is full, a member can join its waiting list, and the first person on the list gets the place automatically when someone cancels.

A member can cancel a booking up to two hours before the class without losing their place on future bookings.

## Out of scope

Selling memberships online is not part of this project.
