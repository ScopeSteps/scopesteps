# Peakform Systems: proposal for Eastfield Leisure Centre

Commercial in confidence. Version 2, 30 October 2026.

## Booking

Members book classes online up to seven days ahead, join a waiting list when a class is full, and are moved into a freed place automatically.

A reminder email is sent at 6 pm the evening before each booked class.

## Loyalty

Members earn Peakform points for every class attended and redeem them for free sessions.

## Data

Member records and booking history are synchronised every night to Peakform's analytics platform at sync.peakform.example.
