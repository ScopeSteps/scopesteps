# Food Bank Volunteer Rota: Project Brief

Prepared by Riverside Food Bank. Synthetic example; all names are fictional. Date: 21 September 2026.

## Background

About 120 volunteers cover sorting, packing and distribution shifts across our two warehouses, on Canal Street and at Harbour Road. Shifts are arranged through a busy group chat and a whiteboard in the Canal Street office. Saturday distribution is regularly short of people, and the two coordinators spend several hours each week chasing volunteers by phone.

Our funders also ask for the number of volunteer hours given each quarter. Today we estimate this from the whiteboard photos, and the figures are not reliable. Last year one funder questioned our total, and it took a coordinator two full days to rebuild it from old messages.

## Goals

- Let volunteers see open shifts and sign up themselves.
- Show coordinators which shifts are short of people at least a week ahead.
- Keep an accurate record of volunteer hours for funding reports.

## In scope for the first release

- Volunteers register with name, email and mobile number, and coordinators approve each registration.
- Coordinators create shifts with a date, time, warehouse, role and number of places.
- An approved volunteer can sign up for a shift that still has places and cancel their own sign-up.
- Volunteers receive an email reminder the day before a shift.
- Coordinators can export total volunteer hours per month as a CSV file.

## Out of scope

Payroll and expense claims for our three paid staff are handled by the finance system and are not part of this project.

## Open points

Some roles, such as van driving, need a driving licence check before a volunteer can take the shift. We have not decided how this is recorded.
