# Kickoff meeting: Community Garden Plots

Attendees: Helen Marsh (Parks and Allotments Manager, project sponsor), Rob Adeyemi (Millbrook Garden committee chair), Lena Park (web developer), Chris Doyle (product owner). Synthetic transcript; all names are fictional.

**Chris (Product owner):** Thanks, everyone. The aim today is to agree what the first release of the plot system does, and what it does not.

**Helen (Parks):** The waiting list is the big one. People wait two or three years and have no idea where they stand. They should be able to log in and see their position on the list at any time.

**Rob (Committee):** And it has to be fair. Plots go to whoever has waited longest, with no exceptions for friends of the committee. That accusation comes up every year and I'm tired of it.

**Lena (Developer):** That's easy to enforce. The list is ordered by application date, and an offer always goes to the person at the top. Nobody can move someone up by hand.

**Helen (Parks):** One change from the brief. Fourteen days is far too long to sit on an offer; in summer a plot goes to weeds in a fortnight. Offers should expire after 7 days, then move to the next person.

**Rob (Committee):** Some people have asked about splitting a full plot into two half plots, so more people get a go sooner.

**Helen (Parks):** Not in the first release. Half plots change the fee, the site map and the waiting list all at once, so we leave half plots out for now.

**Chris (Product owner):** Noted: half plots are out of scope for release one.

**Lena (Developer):** Could we text people when an offer arrives? Council emails go to spam a lot, and a missed offer means a missed plot.

**Helen (Parks):** Possibly, but texts cost money per message and we have no budget line for it. I'm not agreeing to SMS today; let's see how email goes first.

**Rob (Committee):** What about pensioners and people on benefits? The paper scheme gave them a reduced fee, but nobody remembers how much, and I'm not sure it was ever written down.

**Helen (Parks):** I'll need to check with finance whether the concession continues and at what rate. Leave it open.

**Rob (Committee):** And the neglected plots. Some holders don't turn up for a whole season while people on the list are desperate for space. We should deal with that sensibly.

**Chris (Product owner):** We need an actual rule for that, though: how long before a warning, who decides, and when the plot is taken back. "Sensibly" isn't something Lena can build.

**Helen (Parks):** Agreed, but that's for the committee in spring. One more thing: my team must be able to see every plot holder's contact details, but other plot holders must never see each other's addresses.

**Chris (Product owner):** Good. I'll write that up and send it round. Let's stop there.
